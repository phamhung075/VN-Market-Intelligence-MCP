### Design Decisions

**Period-key scheme:** `cron:<flow-slug>:<YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MMZ>` where `<YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MMZ>` = floor(fire_time) to scheduled boundary. Jitter window ±2min absorbed deterministically. Examples: cowork fires at 14:32Z → `cron:cowork:2026-06-28T14:30Z`; dev-team fires at 14:09Z → `cron:dev-team:2026-06-28T14:07Z`.

**Election layer:** DISPATCHER-LEVEL (one election per tick for the whole cowork dispatcher). Per-slot Step 4.6 claims remain as intra-dispatch dedup within an elected session.

**SF-1 ordering (dev-team):** SF-1 first (session-level overlap guard, TTL=5400s), fire-election second (cross-session tick dedup, TTL=600s). On election LOSS: release SF-1 before EXIT. Independent task_ids → no deadlock.

**Lease:** TTL=600s on all fire-election locks. NO heartbeat (per-fire, no stickiness). Explicit `task_release` at every exit path.

**Activation gate documented in 3 places:**
- `docs/agents/cowork-team/flow/leader-lock.md` header comment
- `.claude/skills/cron-cowork-team/SKILL.md § P3-OBSERVE-ONLY-RETIREMENT`
- `.claude/skills/cron-detect-loop/SKILL.md § P3-OBSERVE-ONLY-RETIREMENT`
- `.claude/skills/dispatch-claim/SKILL.md § Fire-Time Election`

OBSERVE-ONLY conventions remain authoritative FALLBACK until TASK_1995 (P3-QA) passes 3 smoke tests.
