### why-change (from the plan the router proposed)

| code | `ocr_backends.py:540 AutoFallbackOcrBackend` | `ocr_worker.py:425 ocr_pages_worker` |

This ratifies the architect's own EVIDENCE-PROVENANCE FLAG on this row, which was right and cuts the

### two further corrections to the brief, both first-hand
- **The "PID-1-only trim" premise is inverted.** AC-0 ran entirely in PID 1
  (`docker compose run --rm --no-deps -e OCR_TEXT_BACKEND=auto`, `scripts/audits/ocr_bench_inner.py`).

  The genuine unfixed gap on A is that its trim is per-`/extract`-call and per-run-finally, never **per fire**.
- **"Budget 4 is below the measured 6-fire saturation" is true of the hard ceiling and false of the bar.**
  AC-0's own table (`docs/agent-memory/decisions/dev-pdf-extractor-ac0-findings-20260825T1830Z.md`):

  N=14 → 100.00% (1250 events). The ratified headroom bar is ≤80%. It breaks between N=1 and N=3.
  A budget of 4 ported to mechanism A sits above 90% of cap. Any safe A-budget is 1, maybe 2 — at or below
  FPT's natural 1-in-51 rate and far below DBC's 6-in-14, i.e. the rescue is off exactly where it is wanted.

### the reopen clause is an AND, and leg (ii) is untouched
The 2026-08-25T17:52Z closure required **both** (i) the memory sweep coming back FLAT **and** (ii) a recall
proxy achieving `min(legitimate) > max(broken)` with one fixed constant on a frozen, hash-deduplicated n≥8
sample with FPT held out. Leg (ii) was falsified **structurally** — ink coverage divides by all crop
foreground, conflating missed text with non-text ink the engine correctly skipped; at n=2 the bands already
overlap; and AC-0's own incidental finding adds a false-**negative** direction (DBC p12/p15 are genuinely
garbled at confidence 0.5348/0.5859, above the 0.5 threshold, so no rescue fires). No memory result reaches
leg (ii). The AND cannot close today regardless of any re-measure.

### what a re-measure would have to show (recorded, deliberately NOT dispatched)
Port per-fire `malloc_trim(0)` plus a fire budget into `AutoFallbackOcrBackend.recognize_text`, then re-run
the identical AC-0 sweep — same vehicle `DBC_2025_Q4.pdf`, same `report_id 272bfb4e-…`, same thresholds
0.01/0.33/0.36/0.5/1.1, same in-process `/sys/fs/cgroup` read, never `ru_maxrss` — and show `memory.peak`
FLAT within noise across N=0..14 **and** `memory.events.max == 0` at N=14. Anything short of flat-at-N=14
re-kills leg (i). Not opened as a row: worthless until leg (ii) has a candidate proxy, and it would burn an

### the brief's "last surviving track" premise is FALSE — and this is the cycle's real finding
`lang="vi"` is dormant only on mechanism A. `ocr_worker.py:266` constructs
`PaddleOCR(lang="vi", use_angle_cls=False, use_gpu=False)` with **no** `OCR_TEXT_BACKEND` gate, reached from

production defaults**, and is the one Vietnamese-PaddleOCR mechanism that actually executes. It has never been
measured, never been ruled on — and 1db5f9f81 just capped it at 4 fires/document as a memory safety belt,
a number imported from a sweep of a different mechanism in a different process. On a document needing more
than 4, fires 5..N are skipped and those pages ship with sub-30-char text, announced only at WARNING.
That is a quality decision taken without a quality ruling.
→ minted `DECIDE-PDFX-OCRWORKER-PAGE-RESCUE-LIVE-UNMEASURED-QUALITY-PATH` (backlog[594], P1, BLOCKED to 09:00Z,
