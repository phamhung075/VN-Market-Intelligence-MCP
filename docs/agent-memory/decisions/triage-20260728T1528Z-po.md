## Decision

**DECLINE the ack.** No entry added. `.acked_memory[]` is unchanged (still only
`rag-service`). File `docs/data/auditor-launchd-ack.json` is untouched by this
decision — the "one ledger edit described" is explicitly not made.

## Why

The ledger's own contract for `.acked_memory` (its `_comment_acked_memory`
field) gates suppression on the breach being a **legitimately high steady
state** whose elevated level is root-caused as healthy/expected by the tracked
row — that is exactly what was true for `rag-service` (seeded 2026-07-25:
"legitimately-high steady-state ... healthy and serving throughout").

`pdf-extractor` fails that test in the opposite direction. Its own tracking
row (`FIX-PDFX-TESSERACT-CONCURRENCY-VIOLATES-SINGLE-WORKER-INVARIANT`, P0,
BACKLOG, minted 2026-07-28T14:47Z by po on direct read-only evidence) states
the high-memory state is **not** legitimate: a code-declared, code-commented,
unit-tested single-worker OCR invariant
(`ProcessPoolExecutor(max_workers=1)` + `threading.Semaphore(1)`) is being
violated 10x in production (10 concurrent `tesseract`, all `PPID=1`,
re-parented — not in-pool children), accounting for ~890 MiB of RSS that pins
the container at 96-99% of a 2.5 GiB cap with 20-90 MiB headroom, re-armed by
every 15-wide caller burst from mcp-server. That is a named, falsifiable,
**live OOM mechanism**, not a benign plateau.

Acking it would therefore repeat, in substance, the exact shape the prior PO
DECISION on `FIX-AUDITOR-TIER1-A30-MEM-SINGLE-CONTAINER-SCOPE` (recorded in
this same file) explicitly forbade: "static overrides are the same
lagging-whitelist failure mode as `FIX-AUDITOR-D4-WHITELIST-DATA-QUALITY-
ANOMALY-PREFIX` and would also silently swallow a REAL rag OOM approach." An
ack here is a per-container suppression that would silence the fleet's only
memory detector on the one container proven to be sitting on an active,
undispatched OOM mechanism — the sanctioned-mechanism/forbidden-shape
question the router posed resolves to **forbidden shape** for this
container, notwithstanding the surface resemblance to `rag-service`
(both currently plateau-shaped: 15:01-15:24Z samples show pdf-extractor
oscillating 95.45-96.76%, tesseract pool pinned at 10 with turnover, no
monotonic growth this window — but a stable-looking plateau sitting on top
of a proven-broken concurrency guard is not the same claim as a plateau that
*is* the service's normal operating point).

## What this accepts (named explicitly)

- The Tier-1 gate will keep returning `FAILURE` on `_check_mem_creep` and
  re-spawning `system-auditor` on (up to) every ~30min tick until either (a)
  `FIX-PDFX-TESSERACT-CONCURRENCY-VIOLATES-SINGLE-WORKER-INVARIANT` ships, or
  (b) the container's own MemPerc drops under 85% on its own. This is **not**
  a malfunctioning gate — it is the gate correctly and continuously
  re-confirming a real, uncovered, P0-tracked breach. The ~155k-token cost
  per re-confirming cycle is accepted as the price of not going blind on a
  live OOM risk to the sole BCTC-OCR service.
- `FIX-AUDITOR-TIER1-FRESHNESS-CHECK-RELOCATE-TO-SENTINEL` (READY/P2) is
  **not** the fix for this specific churn and is not reprioritised by this
  decision — that row addresses the self-satisfying heartbeat-freshness
  branch on the `ALL_GREEN` path; today's spawns are driven by a genuine
  `FAILURE` verdict, which already forces a spawn unaided regardless of
  heartbeat freshness (per that row's own `defect` text). Conflating the two
  would misdiagnose today's cost.
- I am explicitly blind, by choice, to whether `pdf-extractor`'s plateau
  could itself be read as evidence the 10-tesseract state is "stable enough
  not to be urgent" — the P0 row already rules that framing out
  (RestartCount unbroken, OOMKilled=false, but headroom as low as ~20 MiB
  measured within the last 24h) and this decision does not re-litigate it.
