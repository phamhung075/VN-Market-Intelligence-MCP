## Why change from the plan

**Raised the bar rather than just reopening the row — the v1 bar is gameable, and by our own stopgap.**
The router asked whether the bar needs raising. It does, and the reason is sharper than "one reading
isn't enough": restarting a leaking process at 89% and then reporting "zero OOM in 24h" is a **false
green of the same family as the `docker inspect .State.OOMKilled` false-negative the v1 bar was written
to replace** — it measures the mitigation, not the fix. Hence RAG-MEM-DURABILITY-BAR v2:
- D1 in-VM dmesg (carried from v1, unchanged);
- **D2** one continuous container lifetime, evidenced by an unchanged `.State.StartedAt` at open *and*
  close — a window whose StartedAt moved is not a short window, it is no window;
- **D3** a **positive** plateau criterion (≤0.02 pp/min over the final 12h **and** ≤85% of cap) —
  because a purely negative criterion has now failed to predict this defect three times running;
- **D4** window INVALID (not merely reset) if any mitigation was applied during it;
- **D5** six named evidence fields required on the row, so a prose assertion can no longer pass as a
  certification.

**Declared the current window VOID rather than reset.** Under D4 it is invalid, and the honest
consequence is worth stating plainly: if the service needs a preemptive restart every ~40–80 minutes
to stay alive, it **cannot** produce a valid 24h continuous-lifetime window, and that impossibility is
itself the finding. Nobody should idle until 08-15 waiting to certify.

**Gate row `FIX-QA-OOM-CLASS-AC3-...` P1 → P0** and extended with defects (3) restart-laundering,
(4) negative-only criterion, (5) grandfather-exemption-on-falsified-row. It was minted 06:50Z against
two defects; three more were evidenced live within the next three hours. Its job is to generalise
D1–D5 fleet-wide, not just for rag-service.

**No agent spawned.** PO's boundary is triage, not dispatch. Rows are left dispatch-ready
(`FIX-RAG-EMBEDDER-...` = `REVIEW`/P0/`next_agent:dev-rag-service`) and `.head` left untouched.
