## Overview

Detect MSCI (Morgan Stanley Capital International) inclusion announcements via keyword matching, verify credibility, and cascade HIGH-severity alerts to large-cap watchlist stocks. Unlike insider dump cascades (Task 1278, sector peer cascade), MSCI inclusion targets specific mentioned stocks + large-cap peers across all sectors, not sector peers.

## Architecture Decision

**Layer split:**
1. **Domain** (`cascadeEngine.ts`, `msciDetector.ts`): Pure detection rules, keyword matching, credibility threshold enforcement, static rule definitions
2. **Application** (`cascadeExecutor.ts`): Orchestration helper that composes domain functions + business logic (large-cap filtering, peer identification)
3. **Tests** (`1279a`, `1279b`): Contract-first TDD with RED failing tests, GREEN passing implementation

**Design rationale:**
- Domain rules (MSCI_INCLUSION_RULES) are static, reusable, and testable in isolation
- Credibility threshold (0.7) enforces signal quality; MSCI inclusion is material → require Reuters/Bloomberg/SSC, reject social media
- Large-cap definition uses hardcoded fallback (MWG, KDH, FPT, MSN, VCB, HPG, BID, CTG) if stock-classification.json unavailable
- Unlike insider dump (Task 1278), MSCI inclusion does NOT cascade to sector peers — it's a global bullish signal, not a contagion risk
- Sentiment direction is bullish (positive catalyst); opposite of insider dump (bearish/negative)

## Risk Assessment

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|-----------|
| MSCI keyword too broad → false positives | Medium | Medium | Test with mixed-language articles; require credibility >0.7 to reject noise |
| Market cap threshold outdated | Low | Medium | Use stock-classification.json as SSOT; fallback to hardcoded list; update on watchlist change |
| Credibility map unmapped source | Low | Low | Default to 0.6 (reject); log warning for manual review |
| buildCausalChain coupling | Low | Low | Keep msciDetector pure function; inject via parameter, not global state |
| Sentiment bullish vs insider dump bearish conflict | Very low | Low | Keywords completely separate; no overlap (xả hàng, bán sạch, thoái sạch ≠ nộp danh sách, đáp ứng tiêu chí, chỉ số msci) |
