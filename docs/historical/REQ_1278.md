## Problem Statement

### Why This Matters

Vietnamese stocks with insider leadership dumps (CEO exit sales, thoái sạch vốn) signal **imminent distress**. When a company leadership exits, capital markets interpret this as:

1. **Loss of confidence** in fundamentals (not public yet in BCTC)
2. **Potential insolvency risk** (especially in banking sector where NIM compression + NPL rises create exit pressure)
3. **Cascading sector impact** — if a major bank's CEO dumps, peer banks (VCB → BID/CTG/ACB) face reputational + counterparty risk contagion

### Historical Context

- **Sprint 1272** classified insider dump keywords (xả hàng, bán sạch, thoái sạch) as BEARISH in sentimentClassifier.ts
- **Sentiment signal reaches cascade engine** but currently **no sector-level rules** to propagate insider dumps from individual stock → banking peers
- **Banking sector is VN's systemic risk hub** — one major bank's CEO exit can trigger confidence cascades in peers

### Investment Outcome

Investors receive **proactive banking sector alerts** when a competitor bank's leadership dumps, enabling them to:
- Reduce exposure to distressed banking peers before public announcements
- Understand **causal link** (insider dump → sector systemic risk) not just raw price movement
- Time position exits or hedge entries

## Functional Requirements

### FR-1: Detect Insider Dump News (Sentiment → Domain-Level Signal)

**Description:** Classify news articles containing insider dump keywords (xả hàng, bán sạch, thoái sạch) as BEARISH signals at the **stock level**, propagate to **domain-level** (banking sector) cascade engine.

**Input:**
- News article (Vietnamese text)
- Extracted ticker (e.g., VCB, BID)
- existingSentiment from sentimentClassifier.ts

**Output:**
- Sentiment: `bearish` (already via sentimentClassifier in sprint 1272)
- CascadeSignal: `{ type: "insider_dump", sector: "banking", affectedStocks: [BID, CTG, ACB], severity: "HIGH" }`

**Business Rule:**
- **Keyword weight:** xả hàng=3, bán sạch=3, thoái sạch=3 (already in sentimentClassifier.ts)
- **Minimum confidence:** >0.6 (insider dump is unambiguous when triggered)
- **Sector mapping:** insider dump of **any banking stock** → affects **all VN banking peers** (BID/VCB/CTG/ACB/TCB/MBB)
- **Stock specificity:** only **BID/VCB/CTG/ACB** from watchlist are escalated (per task spec and stock-classification.json)

**DDD Layer:** application (signal propagation logic) + domain (cascadeEngine rule definitions)

---

### FR-2: Cascade Rule: Insider Dump → Banking Sector Alert

**Description:** Add INSIDER_DUMP cascade rule to cascadeEngine.ts that maps insider selling sentiment from one banking stock → alerts on peer banking stocks.

**Input:**
- News event with sector="banking", insider_dump keyword matched
- Original affected stock (e.g., VCB leaked CEO resignation)
- Watchlist banking stocks: BID, VCB, CTG, ACB

**Output:**
- Alert triggered on **peer** banking stocks (not the original stock — already handled by stock-level sentiment)
- Alert severity: HIGH (insider dumps = systemic risk signal)
- Alert body includes: `"CEO insider selling detected at [ORIGINAL_STOCK]. Banking sector peer [PEER_STOCK] flagged."`

**Business Rule:**
- **Contagion principle:** insider dump at one bank signals **systemic banking distress**, so peers must be alerted
- **Affected sector:** banking only (no cross-sector cascade; insider dumps are company-specific exit signals, not macro)
- **Rule name:** `insider_dump_banking_peers`
- **Precedence:** HIGH (supersedes neutral alerts, queues immediately)
- **Cooldown:** 30 min per stock (macro cooldown applies; avoid alert fatigue from same insider dump story)

**DDD Layer:** domain (cascadeEngine.ts — rule definition + routing)

---

### FR-3: Integrate Insider Dump Cascade into Intelligence Cycle

**Description:** Wire the insider dump cascade rule into the intelligence cycle's `updateMacroContext()` logic so cascade signals fire during daily briefing/alert generation.

**Input:**
- Macro context with sentiment signals from pollNews step
- Cascade rule registry (INSIDER_DUMP_RULES)

**Output:**
- Queued alerts for peer banking stocks
- Macro context updated with insider_dump cascade metadata (for briefing generation)

**Business Rule:**
- **Trigger point:** after sentiment classification (end of pollNews step A2), before sector peers sync (step C2)
- **Idempotency:** same insider dump story (same date, same original stock) does not re-trigger cascade within 6-hour window
- **Error handling:** if cascade rule fails, log warning + continue (non-fatal)

**DDD Layer:** application (intelligenceCycleJob.ts — cascade orchestration)

## Non-Functional Requirements

### Performance

- **Cascade rule matching:** <50ms per news article (keyword match + rule lookup)
- **Alert generation:** <500ms total for insider dump cascade (peer lookup + queue write)
- **Full cycle impact:** <10% increase in total intelligence cycle time (currently ~30–45s peak)

### Data Freshness

- **Sentiment classification:** real-time (during pollNews, 10–15 min VPS fetch lag)
- **Cascade rule:** applied immediately to classified signals
- **Alerts:** sent to MARKET channel within 2 min of insider dump detection

### Language

- **Sentiment keywords:** Vietnamese (xả hàng, bán sạch, thoái sạch) via sentimentClassifier.ts
- **Alert body:** bilingual (Vietnamese headline from news + English explanation of cascade logic)
- **Acceptance criteria:** tested on mixed Vietnamese/English news articles

### Data Quality Expectations

- **Insider dump keyword false positives:** ~2-5% (e.g., "xả hàng" in unrelated contexts like factory layoff "xả hàng lao động"). Mitigated by confidence threshold >0.6.
- **Banking stock misidentification:** <1% (SSC parser confidence on stock ticker extraction already >99%)
- **Peer list staleness:** updated quarterly via stock-classification.json (currently 5 banks: BID/VCB/CTG/ACB/TCB)

---

## Edge Cases & Data Quality

- [ ] **Empty news article text** → skip cascade (confidence=0, no rule match)
- [ ] **Malformed stock ticker** → log warning, skip cascade for that stock
- [ ] **Insider dump headline + bullish body** (e.g., "CEO dumps shares but company reports record profit") → use headline sentiment (insider action > operational results)
- [ ] **Same insider dump story across multiple news sources** → deduplicate via article hash (existing RAG logic); cascade fires once per 6h per original stock
- [ ] **CEO dump at non-banking stock mixed with banking context** (e.g., "FPT CEO exits; banking sector to be affected") → only fire cascade if **original stock IS banking**
- [ ] **Cascade fires during market closed hours** → queue normally; Alert Commander sends at market open next day
- [ ] **Banking peer not in watchlist** (e.g., TCB/MBB insiders dump but not in BID/VCB/CTG/ACB watch) → silently skip cascade to that peer (per stock-classification.json constraint)
- [ ] **Circular cascade risk** (e.g., A's insider dump → B alert → B cascade → A alert) → prevented by rule condition "only fire if ORIGINAL stock != PEER stock"
