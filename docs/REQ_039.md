# REQ-039: Lớp Bảo Vệ Vốn — Rủi Ro Pháp Lý + Radar Chính Sách + TPDN BĐS

status: APPROVED
sprint: 039
po_vision: Bảo vệ vốn nhà đầu tư bằng cách phát hiện sớm rủi ro pháp lý (khởi tố, bắt giam, truy thu thuế), theo dõi chính sách nhà nước (Công Báo, Thông tư NHNN), và tracking lịch đáo hạn trái phiếu doanh nghiệp BĐS — ba nguồn rủi ro phi thị trường lớn nhất tại Việt Nam.

---

## User Stories

### US-1: Phát hiện rủi ro pháp lý
As a Vietnamese stock investor,
I want the system to automatically detect legal risk keywords ("khởi tố", "bắt giam", "truy thu thuế", "thu hồi giấy phép", "anti-dumping") in any news source,
So that I receive a CRITICAL alert immediately when a legal event threatens a stock in my watchlist — often days before the stock price fully reflects the damage.

### US-2: Radar chính sách nhà nước
As a Vietnamese stock investor,
I want the system to monitor Vietnamese government policy sources (Công Báo, NHNN circulars) for new Nghị định, Thông tư, and Quyết định,
So that I know about regulatory changes affecting my sectors 24–72 hours before mass media reports them.

### US-3: Lịch đáo hạn TPDN BĐS
As a Vietnamese stock investor holding banking stocks (VCB),
I want the system to track corporate bond maturity schedules for real estate developers,
So that I can assess credit risk to banks when large bond tranches come due.

## Functional Requirements

### FR-1: Legal Risk Detector (`src/domain/services/legalRiskDetector.ts`)

- **Description**: Pure domain service. Scan text for Vietnamese legal risk patterns. Return structured `LegalRiskSignal` with risk type, severity, and matched keywords.
- **Patterns** (Vietnamese + English):
  ```
  khởi tố, bắt tạm giam, tạm giam, truy thu thuế, phạt vi phạm thuế,
  thu hồi giấy phép, đình chỉ hoạt động, phong tỏa tài sản, kê biên,
  chống bán phá giá, anti-dumping, thua kiện, bồi thường thiệt hại,
  cưỡng chế thi hành án, điều tra, vi phạm pháp luật, xử phạt hành chính
  ```
- **Output**: `LegalRiskSignal { riskType: LegalRiskType, severity: Severity, matchedPatterns: string[], stockCodes: string[], confidence: number }`
- **Risk types**: `prosecution` | `tax_penalty` | `license_revocation` | `anti_dumping` | `litigation` | `asset_freeze` | `investigation`
- **Severity mapping**:
  - `prosecution`, `asset_freeze` → CRITICAL
  - `tax_penalty`, `license_revocation`, `anti_dumping` → HIGH
  - `litigation`, `investigation` → MEDIUM
- **Pure function**: No I/O. No imports from infrastructure/.
- **Stock resolution**: Uses existing `detectStocksInText()` from `stockAliases.ts`.

### FR-2: Policy Impact Mapper (`src/domain/services/policyImpactMapper.ts`)

- **Description**: Pure domain service. Classify Vietnamese policy documents by type and map to affected sectors/stocks.
- **Policy types**: `credit_policy` | `tax_change` | `industrial_zone` | `energy_policy` | `real_estate_policy` | `trade_policy` | `monetary_policy`
- **Policy-to-sector mapping**:
  | Policy keyword | Affected sectors | Direction |
  |---|---|---|
  | room tín dụng, lãi suất điều hành | banking | context-dependent |
  | thuế TTĐB, thuế nhập khẩu | beverages, automotive | bearish |
  | KCN, khu công nghiệp | industrial_zone | bullish |
  | quy hoạch điện, FIT | energy, renewable | context-dependent |
  | siết tín dụng BĐS | real_estate | bearish |
  | FDI, đầu tư nước ngoài | industrial_zone, technology | bullish |
- **Output**: `PolicySignal { policyType, affectedSectors: DomainType[], affectedStocks: string[], direction: ImpactDirection, summary: string }`
- **Pure function**: No I/O.

### FR-3: Công Báo Fetcher (`src/infrastructure/fetchers/congbao.ts`)

- **Description**: Scrape `congbao.chinhphu.vn` for new Nghị định, Thông tư, Quyết định.
- **Method**: RSS feed if available, otherwise Cheerio HTML scraper.
- **Output**: `PolicyDocument[] { title, documentNumber, issueDate, category, fullTextUrl, summary }`
- **Error handling**: Never throw — return empty array. Use circuit breaker pattern (existing `circuitBreakerRegistry`).
- **Rate limiting**: Use existing `rateLimiter` with `congbao.chinhphu.vn` host config.

### FR-4: SBV Circular Fetcher (`src/infrastructure/fetchers/sbvCircular.ts`)

- **Description**: Scrape NHNN (State Bank of Vietnam) for new Thông tư about credit, interest rates, reserve requirements.
- **Source**: `sbv.gov.vn` regulatory announcements section.
- **Output**: `SbvCircular[] { title, circularNumber, issueDate, effectiveDate, category, summary }`
- **Error handling**: Same pattern as congbao fetcher.

### FR-5: Bond Maturity Tracker (`src/domain/services/bondMaturityTracker.ts`)

- **Description**: Domain service that maintains a calendar of corporate bond maturity dates for real estate developers.
- **Initial data**: Static dataset of known TPDN maturity schedules for major RE developers (VHM, NVL, KDH, DIG, PDR, BCG).
- **Auto-extraction**: Extend `commodityTracker.ts` pattern — auto-extract bond maturity mentions from news text.
- **Output**: `BondMaturityEvent[] { issuer, issuerCode, amount: number, maturityDate: string, status: 'upcoming' | 'due' | 'defaulted' | 'extended' }`
- **Alert trigger**: Generate alert when maturity date is within 30/14/7 days.

### FR-6: Signal Integration

- **Extend `SignalType`**: Add `"legal_risk"` | `"policy_change"` | `"bond_maturity"` to existing union in `signalDetector.ts`.
- **Wire into `pollNews.ts`**: After news normalization, run `detectLegalRisk()` on each entry. If risk detected → create CRITICAL signal.
- **Wire into `cascadeEngine.ts`**: Add `POLICY_RULES` and `LEGAL_RISK_RULES` to cascade engine.
- **Wire into `intelligenceCycleJob.ts`**: Add policy fetch as Step A3 (parallel with existing A/A2).

### FR-7: MCP Tools

- `get_legal_risk_signals(stock?: string, days?: number)` → Recent legal risk events
- `get_policy_signals(sector?: string, days?: number)` → Recent policy changes
- `get_bond_maturity_calendar(months?: number)` → Upcoming bond maturities

---

## Non-Functional Requirements

- All new domain services must be pure (no I/O, no infrastructure imports).
- All fetchers must use circuit breaker + rate limiter patterns.
- All signal types must flow through existing alert pipeline (dedup, cooldown, grouper).
- Tests: minimum 15 tests per domain service, 5 per fetcher, 5 per MCP tool.

---

## Edge Cases

1. **False positive legal keywords**: "khởi tố" in article about competitor, not watchlist stock → stock resolution via `detectStocksInText()` filters this.
2. **Policy retroactivity**: Some Nghị định have retroactive effective dates → store `effectiveDate` separately from `issueDate`.
3. **TPDN restructuring**: Bond maturity extended by issuer agreement → status `"extended"`, not `"defaulted"`.
4. **Duplicate policy alerts**: Same Nghị định reported by multiple sources → handled by existing `alertDedup`.
